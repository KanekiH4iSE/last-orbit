# Last Orbit

A top-down space survival game in the style of Survivor.io. Pilot a shuttle against endless waves of robots, aliens and monsters and last ten minutes until the warp gate opens.

## Gameplay
- Move with WASD or arrow keys (touch: drag on the left half of the screen). Space or the DASH button dashes with brief invulnerability.
- Weapons fire automatically. Collect energy cells to level up and choose one of three upgrades. Reroll twice per run.
- 6 weapons: pulse laser, homing missiles, combat drones, plasma cutter, EMP pulse, arc coil. Max a weapon (level 8) and own its partner upgrade to evolve it.
- 9 passive upgrades. You can carry 6 weapons and 6 passives.
- 13 enemy types across robots, aliens and monsters, elites, swarm events, and three bosses (Mega Mech, Hive Queen, Void Titan). Each boss has twice the health of the last.
- Enemies hit harder every minute.
- Hazards: asteroids that hurt on contact and break apart, meteor showers, and black holes that pull in the ship and enemies.
- Drops: coins, repair kits, magnets, bombs, freeze, shields, and supply crates from elites and bosses.
- Hangar: spend gold on 3 ships and 6 permanent upgrades. A codex lists every enemy. Progress is saved in the browser.
- Endless space background with planets and moons, suns, bright stars, nebulae and black holes.

Single file (`index.html`), no build step, no dependencies. Built with AI assistance (vibe-coded) for Cycle 1, Personal Product.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser.
