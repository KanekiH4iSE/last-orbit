LASTORBIT

Robots, aliens and monsters are swarming your sector. Hold out for ten minutes until the warp gate opens.

Move
WASD or arrow keys. Touch: drag on the left side.
Dash
Space or the DASH button. Brief invulnerability.
Attack
Automatic. Collect energy cells to level up and pick upgrades.
Loot
Coins, repair kits, magnets, bombs, freeze and shields. Elites and bosses drop supply crates.
Evolve
Max a weapon (level 8) and own its partner upgrade.
Hazards
Asteroids hurt on contact. Black holes pull everything in. Enemies hit harder every minute and every boss is twice as tough.
Keys
P pause, M mute, R reroll on level-up.
